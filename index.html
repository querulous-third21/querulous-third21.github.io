❓ What Is Rekey?

Have you ever started typing a message, only to realize you forgot to switch your keyboard layout? You meant to type in English but the letters came out in Russian, or you wanted Ukrainian but got English characters instead. It's frustrating, it's common, and it wastes your time.

Rekey solves this instantly. It lives quietly in your macOS menu bar, and whenever you accidentally type in the wrong layout, simply tell Rekey to fix it. The words magically transform into the language you actually wanted.

  • ⚡ Instant Correction: Fix words in just a couple of clicks. No retyping, no fuss.

🎯 How to Use Rekey

Using Rekey is as simple as it gets. Once it's running, you'll see a small icon (🔄) in your menu bar at the top of your screen.

  1. Type accidentally in the wrong layout: For example, you thought you were typing English but you were actually using the Russian keyboard layout.
  2. Select the misplaced text in any application (Messages, Email, Browser, Documents — it works everywhere).
  3. Click the Rekey icon in your menu bar and choose "Fix Selection" from the dropdown menu.
  4. Boom! Your text is instantly corrected into the right language. That's it!

You can also set up a keyboard shortcut in Rekey's preferences to correct text even faster — no mouse needed at all.

🆘 Troubleshooting & FAQ

❓ Rekey doesn't correct my text. What should I do?

Make sure you have granted Rekey Accessibility permission. Go to System Preferences → Security & Privacy → Privacy → Accessibility, and check the Rekey box. You might also need to quit and reopen Rekey after granting access.

❓ Will Rekey interfere with my other apps?

No. Rekey only reads text when you explicitly ask it to. It stays completely passive in your menu bar until you click it or trigger the shortcut.

❓ Can I use Rekey with any application?

Yes! Rekey works with any app that uses standard macOS text editing — Safari, Chrome, Mail, Messages, Microsoft Word, Pages, Notion, Slack, and just about everything else.

❓ Is my data ever sent anywhere?

Absolutely not. Everything happens locally on your computer. Rekey has zero Internet connection and never sees your text.
